About Wasatch Aquatic Specialties

At Wasatch Aquatic Specialties, we're Central Utah's trusted experts in specialized service and installation for large-scale commercial pools.

Our skilled team excels in maintaining and upgrading complex systems—including UV sanitation, chlorine feeders, boilers, filters, chemical controllers, and winterizing—delivering tailored, top-tier solutions for expansive aquatic facilities across the region.

From routine maintenance to precision installations, we uphold the highest standards of safety, efficiency, and performance, ensuring your pool meets the demands of heavy use while providing patrons with the comfort of safe, clean water.

We pride ourselves on quick response times, addressing your needs promptly to minimize downtime and keep your facility running smoothly. With a commitment to excellence and a passion for perfection, we keep your pool in flawless condition year-round. Trust Wasatch Aquatic Specialties to be your partner in managing pools that go beyond the everyday.
